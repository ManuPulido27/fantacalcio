# Fantacalcio ASTA Strategy 2025/26

## Budget Allocation (500 total credits)
- Strikers (A): 50% → ~250 credits
- Midfielders (C): 25% → ~125 credits
- Defenders (D): 20% → ~100 credits
- Goalkeepers (P): 5% → ~25 credits

## Player Selection Criteria

### General
- Prefer players with **highest FVM (Fantavoto Medio)**.
- Prioritize good **FVM / Qt.A ratio** (value for money).

### Role-specific priorities
- **Strikers (A):**
  - Goals (most important).
  - Assists.
  - High FVM / Qt.A ratio.
- **Midfielders (C):**
  - Assists (most important).
  - Goals.
  - High FVM / Qt.A ratio.
- **Defenders (D):**
  - Consistently good FVM.
  - Goals (bonus value).
  - High FVM / Qt.A ratio.
- **Goalkeepers (P):**
  - Clean sheets.
  - Good FVM.
  - Reasonable cost vs alternatives.

## Workflow During ASTA
- I will keep a separate file called **`asta.md`** where I log my buys (player, role, price).
- Each time I buy a player, I will ask the LLM:
  1. To update `asta.md` with the new entry.
  2. To recalculate my remaining budget.
  3. To check whether I am still aligned with my role allocations.
- When I ask about a player being called, the LLM should:
  - Show me his stats (FVM, Qt.A, FVM/Qt.A ratio, plus goals/assists/clean sheets depending on role).
  - Suggest the **maximum price I should bid**, based on my current budget and allocations.
